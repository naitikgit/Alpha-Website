Quality work. Clear choices. Better car care.

Alpha Automation started with a simple idea: looking after a car should be easier to understand. We’re building a modern place for drivers to find quality automotive products, get helpful guidance and arrange the care their vehicles need.

Our range spans more than 100 car-related products, from spare parts and accessories to everyday care essentials. We aim to make every product easier to evaluate with clear specifications and relevant vehicle information.

As we grow, our focus stays on quality work and a professional, straightforward experience from the first question through to the next drive.

  • Quality-led service and product choices
  • Useful details to support confident decisions
  • Automotive help in one considered place
